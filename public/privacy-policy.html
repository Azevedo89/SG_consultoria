Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data controller

SG Consultoria e Assessoria is responsible for the processing of personal data collected through this website. For privacy or data protection questions, contact us at geral@sgconsultoria.pt.

2. Data we collect

We may collect data voluntarily provided through the contact form, including name, email, phone number, subject and message. We may also store the selected language preference locally in the user's browser.

3. Purposes

Data is used to respond to contact requests, review submitted matters, provide information related to requested services, manage communications and ensure normal website operation.

4. Technical providers

Data is not sold. Contact form submissions may be processed by technical providers required for the operation of the website, including FormSubmit, which is used to forward submitted requests to SG Consultoria e Assessoria's email address. The contacts section also embeds Google Maps; Google may collect technical browser data (IP address, device type, cookies) when those maps load, in accordance with Google's own policies.

The website uses the Google Ads platform to measure the performance of advertising campaigns, as described in section 5.

The contact form is processed by the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co), which forwards the submitted content to SG Consultoria e Assessoria's email address.

5. Cookies and measurement technologies

This website uses the Google Ads tag (gtag.js, identifier AW-18280831262) to measure the performance of SG Consultoria e Assessoria's advertising campaigns.

What is collected
When you visit the site, the tag may collect your IP address, device and browser type, the pages visited, the date and time of the visit, the identifier of the ad through which you reached the site (where applicable) and the submission of the contact form as a conversion event. The content of the form is not sent to Google, only the fact that it was submitted.

Cookies used

  • _gcl_au — set by Google, links the visit to the ad that originated it for conversion measurement purposes, with a duration of 90 days.
  • If you accept advertising cookies, Google may additionally set cookies on the google.com and doubleclick.net domains for ad personalisation and remarketing.

Consent
On your first visit a notice is shown where you can accept or decline. By default, and until you make that choice, all consent signals are communicated to Google as denied, with no ad personalisation and no remarketing. Your choice is stored in your browser.

How to withdraw consent
At any time, through the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer, or by deleting the site's cookies and data in your browser settings.

Legal basis
The data subject's consent, under Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation, for advertising and measurement cookies.

Further information on data processing by Google is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Retention

Data is kept only for the period necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, without prejudice to retention periods required by law or needed to protect legitimate rights and interests.

7. Data subject rights

The data subject may request, where applicable, access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing or portability of their data. Requests may be sent to geral@sgconsultoria.pt.

8. External links

This website may contain links to external platforms, including social media. SG Consultoria e Assessoria does not control the privacy practices of those platforms and recommends consulting their respective policies.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated whenever necessary. The version published on this page is the applicable version at any given time.
